GOOD TO KNOW / 06

A little more
clarity.

No surprises. Just a transparent look at how the approved LanXgrow journey is intended to work.

When does my free practice access begin?+

The free practice day begins on your first successful Google login and ends at 11:59 PM India Standard Time that same calendar day, whether or not every activity is used. Further access requires a paid plan or separately granted access.

Can I try the one-minute introduction as a guest?+

Yes. The introductory interview is designed to work without collecting personal details first. To save and access its report, the learner signs in and completes the required profile details.

How does the learning plan adapt?+

The approved approach is Assess → Learn → Practise → Feedback → Improve. AI proposes which skill activity to complete; learners may choose topics inside that activity, and the plan updates from each activity.

Does Video Assessment record or store snapshots?+

The approved formal assessment uses the camera for live presence only, without storing snapshots or screen recordings. Learners must grant camera permission to start that assessment; other practice remains accessible if camera access is declined.

Which features are coming soon?+

Call, English Pro Course and Learn & Entertainment are marked coming soon. The launch scope includes practice, introductory interview, the longer interview and assessment flows, and a dashboard, subject to implementation verification.
