   https://www.mercer.com/insights/talent-and-transformation/talent-assessment/indias-graduate-skill-index-2025/
3. World Economic Forum, Future of Jobs 2025, Skills outlook: empathy and active listening among top ten core skills. Global employer survey across 55 economies; not an India-specific English test.
   https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/3-skills-outlook
