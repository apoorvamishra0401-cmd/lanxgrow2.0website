# LanXgrow: evidence-led next steps (proposals only)

Prepared 24 October 2026. These are not additional homepage features shipped in this release. Research supports the learner need; it does not establish a LanXgrow sales uplift. Choose experiments and measure completed registrations, qualified programme enquiries and eventual paid enrolments, not only animation clicks. Do not show a partner logo or imply endorsement without permission.

## 1. Homepage: a 45–60-second real product walkthrough
Show a genuine English speaking prompt, a learner response, a specific feedback example and the next suggested practice task. Add captions, a transcript, a poster image and a tap-to-play control; no autoplay audio. If the engine is not ready, label the walkthrough as an illustrative preview.

Evidence: OECD, *How 15-Year-Olds Learn English* (20 February 2024), case studies/interviews in Finland, Greece, Israel, the Netherlands and Portugal. Teachers and students wanted more authentic speaking opportunities; exposure outside lessons was unequal. https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/how-15-year-olds-learn-english_a3fcacd5-en.html

Inference: showing what practice actually involves may reduce uncertainty about an unfamiliar English service. Test demo exposure → completed registrations. The OECD did not test marketing videos or conversions, and its case studies are not Indian Gen Z survey data.

## 2. Services / onboarding: goal-specific English pathways
Let visitors choose classroom expression, interviews, workplace conversations or everyday confidence, then show relevant speaking tasks, expected practice effort, available support and confirmed programme inclusions. The current Services sample selector is a small first step; an actual personalised learning plan needs the learning product to exist.

Evidence: Duolingo's English-specific generational analysis used people who began English courses in 2022 in Brazil, Germany and the US. Motivations differed by generation and country; school was prominent for Gen Z. https://blog.duolingo.com/english-learner-motivations/

This is older behavioural evidence, not a recent Indian study. Do not describe one country's motivations as universal. Test goal selection → registration and onboarding completion by goal.

## 3. Learner dashboard: a transparent starting point and progress sample
Provide a short baseline speaking activity and show a dated comparison of similar tasks. Use clearly explained rubrics for comprehensibility, organisation and language use. Do not invent fluency percentages or imply Pearson/CEFR certification. Add a public sample so a prospective learner can understand the feedback before purchase.

Evidence: Pearson/PSB Insights, *Impact of English* (published March 2024; fieldwork October 2023), surveyed 5,000 additional-language English speakers aged 18–64 in five markets; report averages excluded Florida. Only 25% reported confidence across all four workplace English skills. Pearson's GSE materials recommend locating skill level, setting goals and showing progress. These are self-reported survey findings and a provider's framework recommendations, not proof of LanXgrow effectiveness. India was not sampled.
https://plc.pearson.com/en-GB/news-and-insights/news/english-skills-have-life-changing-impact-are-seen-key-unlocking-higher-pay
https://www.pearson.com/languages/why-pearson/the-global-scale-of-english/educators-impact-of-english-research.html

Test whether the sample report improves qualified enquiries; later measure real task improvement using an appropriate assessment design.

## 4. Services / assessment: make the human role visible
Show a real, consented mentor introduction, an example of specific feedback, and exactly when a learner can ask a human for help. State the limits of AI and explain what happens to recordings before requesting a microphone. Only promise response times the team can deliver.

Evidence: British Council, *Human-centred AI: lessons for English learning and assessment* (March 2025). This position paper prioritises learner needs, teachers, accessibility and responsible AI as a supporting tool. It is expert guidance, not an experiment proving sales or speaking gains.
https://www.britishcouncil.org/research-insight/human-centred-ai-lessons-english-learning-assessment

Inference: clarity about support and AI limits may build informed trust. Test sample-feedback engagement → qualified enquiry and monitor support expectations.

## 5. Entertainment / practice: turn watching into speaking
A short original or licensed English clip should lead to a concrete activity: identify a useful expression, say a response aloud and reuse it in a different context. Avoid presenting endless videos or social scrolling as sufficient English practice.

Recent Gen Z evidence: Harmaini (2026), *Incidental English learning through social media and English Proficiency among Indonesian Gen Z*, Computers and Education Open. The university-hosted abstract reports no statistically significant association between self-reported exposure frequency and the measured listening/vocabulary/grammar scores. Cross-sectional, one EFL setting, not a causal trial and not a test of speaking. This is academic research rather than a large-company report.
https://repository.bunghatta.ac.id/id/eprint/3980/
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeo.2026.100358

Inference: structured activities are a stronger product proposition than passive exposure alone. Test activity completion and subsequent comparable speaking tasks; this paper does not prove that our proposed activity works.

## 6. English landing page: current demand, carefully framed
If using a market-demand research card, use a properly attributed English-specific finding and link its methodology. Keep it secondary to the product demonstration and actual programme details.

Recent large-company evidence: Duolingo Language Report 2025, published December 2025, reports English as its most-studied language in 154 countries, from platform data. https://blog.duolingo.com/2025-duolingo-language-report/

This describes Duolingo users, not all learners, Indian Gen Z or willingness to pay. It does not demonstrate that LanXgrow produces results. Test whether the card adds useful understanding rather than visual noise.

## Suggested order
1. Working provider configuration and verified email/SMS delivery.
2. Truthful product walkthrough plus sample feedback.
3. Confirmed programme inclusions and prices/cancellation terms before payment exists.
4. Real learner evidence: permission-based, dated, contextualised examples after actual use. Never fabricate a testimonial or transfer another company's results to LanXgrow.
5. Goal-aware onboarding and measurable progress when the learning app is built.

Research from respected English-learning organisations is more relevant than unrelated revenue rankings. The 2025 and 2026 sources are recent; the 2022–2024 sources are deliberately labelled older. None supplies a reliable numerical forecast of LanXgrow sales growth.
