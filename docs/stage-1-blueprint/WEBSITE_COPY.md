# WEBSITE_COPY.md

**LanXgrow — proposed English copy for existing pages**
Compiled: 18 October 2026

This document proposes the complete English copy for the routes that already exist. For each page it gives (a) the current on-site copy, (b) the proposed copy, and (c) what changed and why. Nothing here has been written to the site.

**Ground rules applied**
- No fear-based messaging; no blame of parents, schools, boards or teachers.
- No fabricated testimonials, statistics, counts, outcomes or guarantees.
- No startup jargon; plain professional English for parents, learners and investors.
- Capabilities that are not built are described as intended/planned, in the same sentence that mentions them.
- No Vedic Maths. No third-party brand names or borrowed interfaces.
- Numbers are used **only** where `RESEARCH_REGISTER.md` marks them publishable, and always with the caveat attached.

**Legend:** ✏️ proposed revision · ➕ new · ✅ keep as-is

## 2. ABOUT

The brief asks About to explain *why LanXgrow exists*, *the communication challenge*, and *the combination of structured learning, human mentorship and AI-supported assessment*; to explain the knowledge-vs-use gap carefully without claiming all Indian learners have it; and to describe how progress could be assessed.

### 2.1 Page intro

**Current:** heading *An education brand built around how people actually learn to speak.* ✅

**Proposed** ✏️
- Eyebrow: **About LanXgrow**
- Heading: **An education brand built around how people actually learn to speak.**
- Supporting: **English Communication changes what a person can do — at school, at work and in the world. LanXgrow exists to make that growth guided, honest and unhurried.**

### 2.2 Why LanXgrow exists

**Current** (two paragraphs) reads well. **Proposed** ✏️ — replace with a careful, non-blaming version:

- Eyebrow: **Why we exist**
- Heading: **Why LanXgrow exists**
- Paragraphs:
  1. **Most people who find English difficult have not lacked effort. More often, they have lacked a comfortable place to speak, and feedback that encourages rather than discourages.**
  2. **In India, children typically study English for seven to ten years at school — yet speaking and listening get comparatively little practice. Reading, writing and speaking are different skills, and fluency will not follow from grammar exercises alone.**
  3. **This is not true of every learner, and it is not the fault of any one school or teacher. It is simply a gap that a focused, person-led programme can help close — and that is the gap LanXgrow exists to work on.**

**Why:** Sentence 2 is the one publishable research-backed statement (`RESEARCH_REGISTER.md` R5). Sentence 3 is the explicit, required hedge — "not true of every learner" — and refuses to blame schools.

### 2.3 How progress is assessed — ➕ new section (this is the page's most important addition)

The brief requires a description of baseline → comparable tasks → feedback → follow-up, **without** promising a timeline.

- Eyebrow: **Measuring progress**
- Heading: **Progress should be visible, not assumed.**
- Body:
  1. **Every learner begins with a baseline — a short spoken task, in their own words. It gives the mentor a clear starting point instead of a guess.**
  2. **Progress is then looked at through comparable tasks: the same kind of speaking situation, revisited over time, so change is easier to see than in a one-off mark.**
  3. **Feedback stays specific and encouraging — what worked, what to practise next, and why.**
  4. **A follow-up assessment closes the loop and sets the next step.**
- Honest note: **We do not promise a fixed improvement timeline. Genuine progress depends on the learner, the starting point and the practice that follows.**

**Why:** Delivers exactly the assessment model the brief asks for, and the closing note satisfies "do not promise a specific improvement timeline without reliable evidence."

➕ **"Is my voice or video recorded during the assessment?"**
**Answer:** *On this website, nothing is recorded. The voice and video assessment shown here is a visual demonstration — no camera or microphone is used. When live assessments are introduced, learners will be told clearly what is captured and how it is used before anything begins.*

**Why:** The progress question is implied by the About page and should be answerable in the FAQ. The recording question is the single most likely privacy concern and must have an explicit, honest answer.
