01 / Interview simulation

AI Face-to-Face Interview Simulation

10minute practice window
The Format

A timed 10-minute automated video and audio mock interview window.

The Mechanics

Contextual questions open progressively. Candidates are given exactly 90 seconds per question to structure, control, and deliver their response.

The Goal

Designed to train candidates on pacing under time pressure, vocabulary choices, and building professional fluency without mid-sentence hesitation.

Frequently Asked
Questions

How does LanXGrow help me prepare for real-world interviews and assessments?+

LanXGrow gives you structured practice for AI-led and face-to-face interview scenarios, operational interview questions, and communication assessments. It is a space to practise expressing your ideas, responding under time pressure, and learning from feedback before the real opportunity.

Preparation takes time. Regular, thoughtful practice helps you build familiarity and work on the areas that need attention, rather than treating readiness as a one-day task.

Our focus is helping you prepare for these interview and assessment stages. Final selection also depends on the employer’s requirements, later conversations with managers or leadership, background checks, and other hiring decisions. Our commitment is to purposeful preparation and useful feedback, while selection remains the employer’s decision.

Is the introductory 1-minute practice tool fully free?+

The one-minute assessment is planned. This page contains an illustrative journey and practice framework; it does not currently provide a live assessment or generate a performance report. Sign in to review the experience available to your account.

How does the system target my specific career domain?+

Use the Technical and Non-Technical filters on this page to explore example career areas. Account-based question banks and personalised career-track settings are planned; these public filters do not configure an assessment.

Is LanXGrow officially associated with corporate evaluation tools like Versant or Amcat?+

No. LanXGrow is an independent simulator and has no official affiliation, sponsorship, endorsement, or commercial relationship with corporate platforms like Versant, Amcat, Mettl, TestGorilla, or their respective owners. Mention of these platforms is informational and does not establish scoring equivalence or validated compatibility.

Can I toggle between Technical and Non-Technical tracks once registered?+

You can explore both tracks using the filters on this page. Changing saved career preferences inside a learning account is a planned capability, rather than a feature demonstrated here.
